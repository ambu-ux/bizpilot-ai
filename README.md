BizPilot AI – Powered by Ambusise Amanyange

 Project Overview
BizPilot AI** is an enterprise-grade, AI-powered workplace productivity and business operations assistant. Developed for Ambusise Amanyange Charcoal (Pty) Ltd (Registration: `2026/312659/07`), the platform is tailored to empower South African entrepreneurs, SMEs, startups, and professionals. By combining intelligent document generation, live AI assistance, and seamless task management into a single integrated dashboard, BizPilot AI automates repetitive administrative tasks, optimizes workflows, and drives sustainable business growth.

---

Features Implemented
AI Assistant & Chatbot:** Real-time conversational AI interface with custom prompt suggestions and dynamic responses for business strategy and planning.
* **Smart Email Generator:** Instantly drafts professional workplace emails across multiple tone settings (Formal, Friendly, Persuasive, Professional).
* **Meeting Assistant:** Summarizes raw meeting transcripts, extracts core decisions, identifies deadlines, and structures actionable responsibilities.
* **Task Planner / Scheduler:** Interactive Kanban board and scheduling tools featuring AI-driven task prioritization.
* **Quotation & Invoice Generators:** Professional, branded document builders featuring automatic 15% VAT calculations, auto-incrementing invoice numbers, 30-day payment terms, and 10% late fee notices.
* **Funding Proposal Generator:** Investor-ready and grant-ready proposal builder featuring multiple templates (Startup, Green Innovation, Youth Enterprise) and a live **Funding Readiness Score**.
* **Marketing Studio:** Generates multi-channel social media copy, WhatsApp promotions, and Google ad campaigns specifically for sustainable biomass and charcoal products.
* **Analytics & KPI Dashboard:** Visual performance indicators, productivity tracking, and AI usage statistics.
* **Responsible AI Safeguards:** Permanent footer disclaimers promoting ethical AI use across all screens.



Technologies and Tools Used
* **Frontend:** React, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, HTML5, Single-Page Application (SPA) architecture.
* **Backend & AI Integration:** Supabase / Lovable Cloud Edge Functions integrated with Large Language Model (LLM) APIs for dynamic, non-generic AI generation.
* **Design System:** Custom dark mode and light green accent styling (`#2E7D32` primary dark green, `#4CAF50` light green, `#121212` dark background) inspired by Microsoft Copilot and Notion.
* **Development Platform:** Lovable AI.
